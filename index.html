Business Insights Panel
AI-Generated Strategic Insights from Customer Feedback Analysis
Key Findings from Data
Data-Driven
Largest Segment: Cluster 0 (39.47%)
8,932 customers form the largest segment. Their reviews focus on general apparel attributes: color, love, sweater, look, shirt, fabric, soft. This group values product variety, visual appeal, and fabric quality — the core brand experience.
Critical Pain Point: Sizing & Fit (Cluster 1)
3,961 customers (17.51%) focus on sizing issues with keywords: size, small, fit, run, ordered, large, medium. This cluster likely drives the highest return rates and customer dissatisfaction. It represents the single biggest improvement opportunity.
Satisfaction Drivers: Jeans & Dresses
Clusters 2 (Jeans, 12.48%) and 3 (Dresses, 17.84%) show strong satisfaction with positive keywords: great, love, comfortable, perfect, beautiful, flattering. Together they represent 30.32% of all customers. Dresses alone account for 6,145 reviews — the most reviewed product class.
Department Performance Gap
Bottoms (4.28/5) and Intimate (4.27/5) outperform Tops (4.16/5) and Dresses (4.14/5) despite Tops having 2.6x more reviews. Trend department scores lowest (3.84/5) with only 118 reviews — a niche that may need repositioning.
ML Model Performance
Logistic Regression (82.10%) outperforms Random Forest (78.72%) in overall accuracy. LR also shows better macro F1 (0.57 vs 0.39), especially for minority classes. RF achieves near-perfect positive recall (1.00) but fails on neutral (0.04 recall) — essentially ignoring minority classes.
Strategic Recommendations
Actionable
🔴 URGENT: Address Sizing Issues
17.51% of customers talk about sizing problems. Implement: (1) Enhanced size guides with body measurements, (2) Customer-reported "true-to-size" voting system, (3) Size recommendation engine using customer profile data. Target: Reduce sizing-related negative reviews by 40%.
🟢 Double Down on Quality & Fabric
Cluster 0 (39.47%) cares about fabric and quality. Highlight material composition, care instructions, and quality certifications in product listings. Add "fabric feel" ratings to product pages. This addresses the largest customer segment.
🔵 Expand Successful Categories
Jeans (avg rating 4.35) and Dresses (6,145 reviews) are star performers. Expand jeans line with more comfort-focused options. For dresses, leverage "beautiful" and "flattering" messaging in marketing campaigns.
🟡 Invest in Color & Style Variety
Multiple clusters mention "color" as a top keyword (Clusters 0, 2, 4). Offer more color variants for bestselling items. Use cluster-specific language: "cute" for tops shoppers, "flattering" for dress shoppers, "comfortable" for jeans buyers.
🔷 Deploy LR Model + Address Class Imbalance
Deploy Logistic Regression for real-time sentiment monitoring. Address class imbalance (77% positive vs 10% negative) with SMOTE or class weighting to improve neutral/negative detection. Current neutral recall is only 0.21.
